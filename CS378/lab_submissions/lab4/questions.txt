NS3 WiFi Infrastructure Lab – Questions
========================================

Read problemstatement.md first for lab instructions, NS3 background, and
how to generate and explore your trace.

All questions below are graded (1 mark each, 20 marks total).
Write answers in answer.txt as  Qn=<answer>.

** Q1–Q4, Q8  →  use ns3-ap.pcap  **
** Q5–Q7, Q9–Q13  →  use ns3-sta.pcap **

For Q14–Q20 write just the option letter (A, B, C, or D).
For Q8 round to the nearest whole second.
For Q13 give the answer in milliseconds, rounded to the nearest integer.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q1.  How many total packets (frames) are in ns3-ap.pcap?

Q2.  How many 802.11 BEACON frames are in ns3-ap.pcap?
     Filter: wlan.fc.type_subtype == 8

Q3.  How many total MANAGEMENT frames (type == 0) are in ns3-ap.pcap?

Q4.  How many total CONTROL frames (type == 1) are in ns3-ap.pcap?

Q5.  How many total DATA frames (type == 2) are in ns3-sta.pcap?

Q6.  How many UDP packets are in ns3-sta.pcap?
     Filter: udp

Q7.  What is the on-wire frame length (in bytes) of the FIRST UDP data
     frame in ns3-sta.pcap?
     Hint: tshark -r ns3-sta.pcap -Y udp -T fields -e frame.len | head -1

Q8.  How long (in whole seconds) is the ns3-ap.pcap trace?
     Hint: tshark -r ns3-ap.pcap -T fields -e frame.time_relative | tail -1

Q9.  How many total packets (frames) are in ns3-sta.pcap?

Q10. How many unique MAC addresses appear in ns3-sta.pcap?
     (Include ff:ff:ff:ff:ff:ff if present; counts as one address.)
     Hint:
       tshark -r ns3-sta.pcap -T fields -e wlan.sa -e wlan.da | \
         tr '\t' '\n' | sort -u | grep -vc '^$'

Q11. How many ASSOCIATION REQUEST frames are in ns3-sta.pcap?
     Filter: wlan.fc.type_subtype == 0

Q12. How many ASSOCIATION RESPONSE frames are in ns3-sta.pcap?
     Filter: wlan.fc.type_subtype == 1

Q13. What is the beacon interval in milliseconds (rounded to nearest integer)?
     Hint: tshark -r ns3-sta.pcap -Y 'wlan.fc.type_subtype==8' \
               -T fields -e wlan.fixed.beacon | head -1
     1 TU = 1.024 ms.  Multiply the TU value by 1.024 and round.
