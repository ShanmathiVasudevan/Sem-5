## Logistics
- Lab staggering: The class is spilt into Batch A (1400 - 1525 hrs) and Batch B (1530 - 1655 hrs) for each lab.
- Evaluation:
    - Best 7 out of 8 inlabs: 7x4 = 28%
    - Attending all 8 inlabs: 2%
    - Lab midsem: 35%
    - Lab endsem: 35%
- Missing a single lab doesn't require documentation. For missing others, valid documented reasons (pink slip or institute approved reason) will be applicable
