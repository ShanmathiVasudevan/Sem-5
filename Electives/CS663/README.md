## Logistics
- Class Participation: around 5%
- Assignments: 3 assignments, total around 25%
    - Programming based - alone or group of 2 students
    - Viva based evaluation
- Course project: around 20%
    - Viva based evaluation; students choose topic, relative grading
- Exams
    - Midsem: around 25%
    - Endsem: around 25%
Note: "around" means $\pm 10$ %
Slides and announcements on Moodle.
